A few practical notes that apply to almost every modern shooter:

- **Resolution is the single biggest performance lever.** Dropping from 1440p to 1080p often buys more frames than turning every effect to low.
- **Upscaling is your friend.** If the game supports a dynamic resolution or upscaling option, enable it before you start gutting visual quality.
- **VRAM matters more than raw power at high resolutions.** A card that's fast but memory-starved will hitch when textures stream in.
- **Don't ignore the CPU.** Shooters with lots of enemies and effects can bottleneck on a single thread even when your GPU is idling.

## Tuning Performance: The Settings That Move the Needle

Once you're in-game, the options menu can feel like a wall of switches. The table below ranks common settings by how much performance they typically return, so you can spend your budget where it counts.

| Setting | Visual Impact | Performance Cost | Recommended Approach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Resolution / render scale | High | Very high | Lower this first if you're struggling |
| Shadows | Medium | High | Medium is usually the sweet spot |
| Volumetric fog and light shafts | Medium | High | Drop to low on older GPUs |
| Ambient occlusion | Low to medium | Medium | Low is barely noticeable in motion |
| Anti-aliasing | Medium | Medium | Use the cheaper method if offered |
| Motion blur | Subjective | Low | Off if you want clarity |
| Field of view | High (comfort) | Low | Raise it — comfort beats a frame or two |
| Texture quality | High | VRAM-bound | Match to your VRAM, not your GPU tier |
| Frame cap / V-sync | None | None | Cap slightly below refresh for consistency |

Beyond the menu, these habits fix a surprising number of performance complaints:

1. **Update your GPU driver.** A fresh driver is the most common cure for stutter in newly released titles.
2. **Turn off overlays.** Discord, capture tools, and launcher overlays all steal frames and can cause conflicts.
3. **Cap your frame rate.** An uncapped frame rate makes your GPU work hardest on menu screens, which spikes temperatures and fan noise.
4. **Verify your game files.** Corrupted installs cause stutter that no settings change will fix.
5. **Close background apps.** Browsers with dozens of tabs are a genuine frame-rate tax.

Community reports consistently point to the same pattern: most "unoptimized" complaints come from a single setting left at maximum, not from the game being unplayable on modest hardware.
